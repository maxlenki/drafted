Drafted — Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 September 2026

Drafted does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no account, no sign-up, no email address and no login. The app does not ask who you are and has no way of knowing.

What stays on your device

The names players type in are held on your device only. They are never sent anywhere. Deleting the app removes them.

Network

Drafted works with no internet connection. The only time it reaches the network is when you choose to share a result, at which point your phone's own share sheet passes the text to whichever app you pick. We do not see it and we do not keep a copy.

Children

Drafted is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children. There are no in-app purchases, no chat, and no user-generated content that other people can see.

Analytics and advertising

There is no analytics and no advertising in Drafted. If either is ever added, this policy will be updated before that version ships.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
